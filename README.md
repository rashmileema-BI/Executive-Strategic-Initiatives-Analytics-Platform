### Power BI Dashboard Pages

#### 1. Executive Overview Dashboard

Designed to provide executive leadership with a centralized view of strategic initiative performance, operational risks, and organizational progress.

**Features**

* Total Strategic Initiatives KPI
* Completed Projects KPI
* Delayed Projects KPI
* Average Completion % KPI
* Critical Risks KPI
* Top Operational Bottleneck KPI
* Quarterly Completion Rate KPI
* Strategic Initiative Progress Distribution
* SLA vs Due-Date Performance Analysis
* Interactive Filtering by Department, Status, Priority, Owner, and Risk

**Business Impact**

Provides leadership teams with real-time visibility into project execution, strategic progress, and organizational risk exposure.

---

#### 2. Operational Performance Dashboard

Designed to evaluate departmental performance, project execution efficiency, and operational risk management.

**Features**

* Project Completion Rate by Department
* Highest Performing Department Identification
* Most Critical Department Identification
* Overdue Projects Monitoring
* Critical Risk Progress Gauge
* Escalation Status Analysis
* High-Risk Initiative Tracking
* Overdue Projects by Dependency Matrix
* Risk Trend Analysis
* Project Performance Analysis

**Business Impact**

Enables PMOs, project managers, and department heads to identify bottlenecks, monitor project health, and prioritize corrective actions.
